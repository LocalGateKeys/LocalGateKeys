How the 4-item limit works

Every kind of item counts: logins, passkeys, notes, cards, identities, documents and the rest. Items in the trash do not.

Nothing is ever taken away. If you have more than 4 items without Pro, for example after upgrading from an older version or after removing a licence, every item stays usable, editable and fillable. Only adding a new one waits until you buy Pro or go back under 4. Imports bring in as many items as you have free places, and tell you how many before they start.

Sync, two-factor codes and documents

These are part of Pro. Without Pro, two-factor codes and documents you already saved keep working: you can see, copy, fill and export them, and remove them. Only adding or changing them, and syncing, need Pro. Two-factor keys in an import are kept as hidden fields, so nothing is lost.
