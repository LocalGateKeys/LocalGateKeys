How the 7-item limit works

Every kind of item counts: logins, passkeys, notes, cards, identities, documents and the rest. Items in the trash do not.

Nothing is ever taken away. If you have more than 7 items without Pro, for example after upgrading from an older version or after removing a licence, every item stays usable, editable and fillable. Only adding a new one waits until you buy Pro or go back under 7. Imports bring in as many items as you have free places, and tell you how many before they start.

Sync, two-factor codes and documents

These are part of Pro. Without Pro, two-factor codes and documents you already saved keep working: you can see, copy, fill and export them, and remove them. Only adding or changing them, and syncing, need Pro. Two-factor keys in an import are kept as hidden fields, so nothing is lost.

Pay once, keep every update

Pro is not a subscription. You pay US$29.99 once, and every future version of LocalGateKeys keeps Pro, with every new feature included. We will never charge for an update. There is no account or activation server.

Buying

Orders are handled by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. They take the payment, charge the right tax for your country and send the receipt. We never see your card details.

Your licence key appears on screen as soon as the payment goes through; see your Pro licence. If you change your mind, you can get a full refund within 30 days; see the refund policy.

A Pro licence is for one person, on all the PCs they use. See the terms of sale and the licence agreement.

On iPhone, buy Pro in the app through the App Store; the app sends only the App Store receipt to our licence server. The purchase also unlocks LocalGateKeys for Windows: pair your PC with the iPhone and a free PC becomes Pro, or copy the key from the iPhone's Settings and install it on the PC.

Questions about the price

Will I ever pay again?

No. There is no subscription and no paid upgrade. Every update is free.

I already bought Pro. What changes?

Nothing. You keep lifetime Pro and every future update, free. Thank you for being early. Read the announcement.

Does one purchase cover my phone and PC?

Yes: pair your devices and the licence travels with the sync; on Windows you can also enter the key.

Why is there no monthly plan?

Your vault never touches our servers, so there is nothing for a subscription to pay for.
