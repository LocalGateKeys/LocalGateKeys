Everything it does, and where it does it

All of this runs on your own devices. Free holds up to 4 items; Pro removes the limit and adds sync, two-factor codes and documents.

The vault

Encrypted on your disk
Everything you save is encrypted with a key protected by your master password, using Argon2id and AES-256-GCM. Names, usernames and websites are encrypted too, not just passwords.
More than passwords
Website and desktop app logins, passkeys, payment cards, bank accounts, identities, secure notes, documents, SSH keys, API keys, Wi-Fi networks, bookmarks, software licences, subscriptions, warranties and purchases, each with its own fields.
Documents (Pro)
Keep files such as scanned documents in the vault, up to 25 MB each. Each file is encrypted with its own key. You can preview a document, save a copy, or open it in another app.
Two-factor codes (Pro)
Store a site's setup key and LocalGateKeys shows the current code at the top of the login, and on a One-time codes card on the Home page. Codes follow the TOTP standard (RFC 6238): 6 to 8 digits, 15 to 120 seconds, SHA-1, SHA-256 or SHA-512.
Add a code from a QR code
Snip the QR code on the site's two-factor setup page with Win+Shift+S and paste it, or hold it up to your webcam. A Google Authenticator export QR code works too. The image is read on your PC and never saved.
Password generator
Random passwords of 8 to 256 characters, or passphrases of 3 to 16 words.
Password health
See which saved passwords are weak, reused, more than a year old or missing a two-factor code. Breach checks against Have I Been Pwned are off until you turn them on, run only when you start them, and send only five characters of a hash, never the password.
Site logos and kind icons
Each item shows the site's icon, a built-in logo for well-known sites, or an icon for its kind, so you can spot it at a glance.
Search and collections
Find items with a command palette (Ctrl+K), filter chips, favourites and your own collections. Arrow keys, Enter and Ctrl+C work in the item list straight from the search box.
Trash and undo
Deleted items go to the trash, and the message that confirms it has an Undo button. The trash empties itself after 30 days, or keeps everything until you empty it.
Password history
When a login's password changes, the last five old passwords are kept in its encrypted history.

Moving in and out

Import from other password managers
Bring in exports from 1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass, Dashlane, Proton Pass, KeePass and KeePassXC (including .kdbx files), Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox and Safari, or any other CSV file. You review every item before anything is written, and the import never goes online.
Export
Export to an encrypted KeePass KDBX 4 file, or to Bitwarden-compatible CSV or JSON, or KeePass XML. Every export asks for your master password first.

Filling sign-ins

Chrome and Edge extension
Fill and save logins on websites, including sign-ins that ask for the username and password on separate pages. The extension asks the app on your PC for logins that match the exact site, through a local helper. It also fills two-factor codes, addresses and payment cards.
Save prompts you control
After you sign in, the extension offers to save a new login or update a changed password. You can turn the prompt off for any site from the toolbar button.
You start every fill
Nothing is filled until you pick an account. Forms are not submitted for you unless you turn on "Sign in after filling" in Settings, and then only after a fill you started.
Passkeys
When a website in Chrome or Edge offers a passkey, LocalGateKeys can save it in your vault and sign you in with it. You approve every passkey in the desktop app, with Windows Hello or your master password, and the browser's own passkey dialog is always one click away. The private key never leaves the app.
Windows app fill
Fill sign-in windows of Windows programs with a fill button beside the password field or a hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+Space). Each app is bound once, and fill is refused if the program, its window or its fields have changed since.
Quick access
Search your vault and copy from anywhere with a hotkey (Ctrl+Alt+K by default). Ctrl+Alt+L locks the vault at once.
No keylogging, ever
LocalGateKeys installs no keyboard hook, reads no screen content to find passwords, and injects nothing into other programs. If an app does not expose proper sign-in fields, it is not filled.

Unlocking

Master password
At least 12 characters, never written to disk. It is the one thing that opens your vault, and we cannot reset it.
Windows Hello
Unlock with your fingerprint, face or PIN through Windows Hello, after you set it up in LocalGateKeys.
Camera check
An optional webcam face check for PCs without Windows Hello. It is a convenience, not certified anti-spoofing; prefer Windows Hello when you can.
Recovery phrase
An optional 24-word phrase that can open the vault if you forget the master password. It is shown once and never stored by the app.
Auto-lock and clipboard clearing
The vault locks after a period you choose, and copied passwords are cleared from the clipboard. Only what LocalGateKeys copied is cleared.

Backups

Scheduled encrypted backups
Daily or weekly, at a time you set. Each backup is a full encrypted file, checked after writing, with attachments included.
Extra copy in a folder you choose
Have each backup copied to a OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox or Proton Drive folder, or anywhere else. Only encrypted backup files are copied, never the live vault.
Encrypted transfer packages
Move selected items to another vault in an encrypted file protected by its own password.

Sync between your devices (Pro)

Over your home Wi-Fi
Pair a PC once with a one-use QR code (between PCs you paste its text). When both are unlocked on the same network, they exchange changes directly over an authenticated, encrypted connection. Nothing goes through the internet.
Through your own cloud folder
If your PCs are rarely on the same network, sync through a OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive or iCloud Drive folder you already use, protected by a sync password. The provider only ever stores encrypted files. You can use both routes.
What syncs
Website and desktop app logins, and other items such as notes and bookmarks. Collections, documents and attachments do not sync yet. Edits made on two PCs at once keep both versions, and a delete moves the item to the trash on the other PCs.
iPhone
The iPhone app syncs with your Windows PC. It is in public beta: join it through TestFlight.

Privacy

No account
You never sign up or sign in. A Pro licence is a key you keep, checked on your PC.
No telemetry
The app sends no usage data, crash reports or analytics.
Strict offline mode
On by default. It blocks background internet requests and breach checks, and an update check asks you first.
Website icons, your choice
To show a site's icon, the app downloads it from that site. This tells the site you have an account there, and nothing else. It is off by default: turn on "Show website icons" in Settings (strict offline mode must be off).
Update checks you start
Checking for updates downloads one small signed file. The app never downloads or installs anything by itself.
