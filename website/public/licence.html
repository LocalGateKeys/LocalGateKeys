Your Pro licence

Your licence is a signed key. The app checks it on your PC; there is no account and no activation server.

Where you get it

As soon as your payment goes through, the checkout takes you to a page that shows your licence key, which starts with LGK1-, and a licence file to download. Save both somewhere you keep important documents, or simply bookmark the page.

The page may take a minute to show the key while the payment is confirmed. It refreshes by itself.

Turn on Pro

  1. Open LocalGateKeys and unlock your vault.
  2. Go to Tools > Licence.
  3. Paste the key and choose Install, or choose Open file and pick the licence file. Pro turns on straight away, without going online.

Do the same on each PC you use.

If you lose it

  • Reinstalling or restoring a backup? Once entered, the licence is also kept inside your encrypted vault. Unlock the vault, or restore a backup of it, and Pro comes back by itself.
  • Still have the page? Open the bookmark or link from your purchase; it shows the key again.
  • Have your receipt? Enter its order number (it starts with txn_) and the email you bought with at licenses.localgatekeys.app/recover to open your licence page again.
  • Otherwise, email support@localgatekeys.app from the address you bought with, with your order number from the Paddle receipt (you can find it at https://paddle.net). We will send you the key again.

We can only look up a purchase by the email address and order number. We cannot see anything in your vault.

Changed PC?

Enter the same key on the new PC, or restore a backup of your vault there. There is nothing to deactivate on the old one.
