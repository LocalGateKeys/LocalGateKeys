Get started

Install the Windows app, create your vault, then add the browser extension. It takes about five minutes, and you never need an account. Add the iPhone app whenever you like.

  1. 1. Install the app
  2. 2. Create your vault
  3. 3. Add the browser extension
  4. 4. Bring your passwords
  5. 5. Fill Windows apps
  6. 6. Turn on Pro
  7. 7. Add your iPhone

1 Install the Windows app

LocalGateKeys runs on Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit, and comes from the Microsoft Store:

  • Microsoft Store. The Store installs it and keeps it up to date. Open LocalGateKeys in the Microsoft Store, choose Get, then Open.
  • Used the old installer? Uninstall it in Windows Settings › Apps (Installed apps on Windows 11, Apps & features on Windows 10), then install from the Microsoft Store. Your vault stays where it is, and the Store app opens it with the same master password.

Get it from the Microsoft Store Other downloads

2 Create your vault

  1. Open LocalGateKeys. On first start it asks you to create a vault, then a short setup guide offers Windows Hello, import, a recovery phrase and the browser extension. Skip any step and finish it later from the Finish setting up card.
  2. Choose a master password of at least 12 characters. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe. We never have it, so nobody can reset it for you.
  3. Open Security center in the sidebar and set up a recovery phrase. Keep it offline, for example on paper. It opens the vault if you forget the master password.
  4. In the same place, turn on Windows Hello if your PC has a fingerprint reader, a Hello camera or a PIN, so day-to-day unlocking is quick.
  5. Turn on scheduled backups in Settings and choose where the encrypted backup files go, such as a USB drive or a folder you already back up.

3 Add the browser extension

The extension fills sign-ins in your browser. It talks only to the LocalGateKeys app on the same PC, so install the app first.

  1. Add the extension:
    • Microsoft Edge: open it in Edge Add-ons and choose Get.
    • Google Chrome: open it in the Chrome Web Store and choose Add to Chrome.
    • Mozilla Firefox (Windows): open it in Firefox Add-ons and choose Add to Firefox.
  2. Pin it: open the browser's extensions menu (the puzzle-piece icon) and pin LocalGateKeys to the toolbar.
  3. Keep the app running and unlocked. Go to a sign-in page you have a login saved for: the extension offers only the logins saved for that exact site. Nothing fills until you click.
  4. Sign in somewhere new and the extension offers to save the login.

Extension says it cannot reach the app? In the app, open Devices & connections and choose Repair bridge, then restart the browser.

4 Bring your passwords

Choose Import & export in the sidebar and pick an export file from 1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass, Dashlane, Proton Pass, KeePass or KeePassXC, Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox or Safari, or any CSV. You review every item before anything is saved, and nothing goes online. Delete the export file afterwards, because most exports hold passwords in plain text.

The free edition holds up to 7 items. Pro removes the limit.

5 Fill Windows apps

For programs such as game launchers, open a desktop login in the app and choose App binding… to bind it to the program's sign-in window once. After that, a fill button appears beside the password field, or you can press Ctrl+Shift+Space. LocalGateKeys refuses to fill if the program or window has changed since you bound it.

6 Turn on Pro

Pro is a single payment that adds unlimited items, sync between your devices, two-factor codes and documents. After you buy it, choose Install in LocalGateKeys on your licence page, or paste your licence key in Settings › Plan & sync › Licence. See your Pro licence.

7 Add your iPhone

The iPhone app keeps its own encrypted vault and syncs with your PC. Sync is part of Pro.

  1. Install LocalGateKeys from the App Store and open it.
  2. Tap Unlock. The vault opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode, so there is no password to create. A short setup offers import, an optional app password and AutoFill.
  3. Turn on AutoFill: in LocalGateKeys choose Settings › AutoFill › Open AutoFill settings, then turn on LocalGateKeys under General › AutoFill & Passwords.
  4. Pair it with your PC: on the PC open Sync & devices, turn on Wi-Fi sync and choose Show QR code. On the iPhone open the Sync tab and tap Scan QR code. Check that both screens show the same code, then choose Accept on the PC.
  5. Apple Watch (Pro): install LocalGateKeys from the iPhone's Watch app, then open a login with a two-factor code and tap Show on Apple Watch.

Bought Pro on the iPhone? Once paired, a free PC takes the licence over sync and becomes Pro. Help for every iPhone screen is in the user guide.

Mac: get the app from the Mac App Store. Android: the app is coming. It is not available yet.
