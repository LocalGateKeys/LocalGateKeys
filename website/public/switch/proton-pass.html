Switch from Proton Pass to LocalGateKeys

LocalGateKeys keeps your vault on your own devices, with no account and no server. Moving from Proton Pass takes a few minutes.

Pro is US$29.99 once. Free for up to 7 items, with no time limit.

  • No account
  • No server holds your vault
  • How it is secured: security documentation

Why people leave Proton Pass

  • January 2026: Proton Pass added biometric unlock for Plus plans and above, not for the free plan. Source

Facts on this page were checked on 8 November 2026; each one links to its source.

1. Export from Proton Pass

  1. In Proton Pass, open Settings → Export.
  2. Choose CSV, or the unencrypted Proton Pass .zip export, confirm and save the file.

2. Import into LocalGateKeys

In LocalGateKeys, choose Import, pick the CSV file and check the preview. LocalGateKeys recognises the format and maps the columns; nothing is saved until you confirm. Then delete the CSV file, because it holds your passwords unencrypted.

LocalGateKeys reads it as: Proton Pass CSV or Proton Pass export (.zip / JSON)

Passkeys do not transfer in a CSV export; LocalGateKeys imports them from CXF files (Apple Passwords and other apps that export CXF) on PC, Android, iPhone and Mac.

What does not transfer

  • Passkeys, unless they move in a CXF transfer (see the note above).
  • Items in shared vaults or collections you do not own. Export them while you still have access, or ask the owner to.
  • File attachments, because a CSV file holds text only. Save them separately before you close your old account.

What it costs

LocalGateKeys Pro is US$29.99 once. One payment. Every update, forever, free. The free version holds up to 7 items, with no time limit.
