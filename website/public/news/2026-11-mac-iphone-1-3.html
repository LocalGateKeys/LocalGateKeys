LocalGateKeys 1.3 for Mac and iPhone

Version 1.3 of the Mac and iPhone apps is rolling out on the App Store.

Mac

Settings now match Windows and look the same in the main window and with ⌘,. New: a duress password (Pro), a choice of what counts as activity for auto-lock, asking before copying a 2FA code, opening documents stored in the vault in their default app, how many automatic backups to keep, your own quick access shortcut, and Help links. Settings › Backups also holds the backup schedule and the weekly restore test. Mac help

iPhone

New: a duress password (Pro), Wi-Fi sync hosted on the iPhone (Pro) so no PC is needed, answering emergency access requests, export to a CXF file, and a restore test that also checks a computer's full backup. iPhone help
