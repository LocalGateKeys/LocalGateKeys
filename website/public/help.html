Good to know on every device

  • Your vault stays on your devices. There is no account and no LocalGateKeys server holding your passwords.
  • Nobody can reset your master password or app password. Set up a recovery phrase and keep it on paper.
  • Free holds up to 4 items. Pro (US$4.99, paid once) adds unlimited items, two-factor codes, files and sync. Editing, deleting and filling items you already have are never blocked.

First start and profiles

  1. Open LocalGateKeys. Enter a Master password of at least 12 characters, type it again in Confirm password, and choose Create vault.
  2. The Set up LocalGateKeys guide follows, in five steps: create the vault, Set up Windows Hello, Import your passwords, Open recovery phrase (you retype three of its words to prove you saved it), and Test connection for the browser extension.
  3. You can Skip any step. Skipped steps wait on a Finish setting up card in the vault, with Continue setup and Hide.

Tip. To see the guide again: Settings › Help & diagnostics › Show setup again.

Profiles

Each profile is a separate vault with its own master password, for example one for work and one for home.

  • On the unlock screen choose Use another profile, then Create profile and type a Profile name.
  • To edit a profile, press Ctrl+K and run Switch profile. There you can Add profile, Edit, set a Photo… and Accent colour, or Remove it.
  • The tray menu's Lock & switch person locks and goes straight to the profile chooser.

Unlocking and locking

Ways to unlock

  • Master password, then Unlock. Always works.
  • Unlock with Windows Hello: fingerprint, face or PIN. Set it up in Settings › Unlocking › Windows Hello › Set up. Turn on Start Windows Hello automatically to get the prompt as soon as the window opens.
  • Use camera instead: face matching with an ordinary webcam, done on the PC. It is less secure than Windows Hello. Set it up with Set up camera verification.

Forgot your master password?

  1. On the unlock screen choose Forgot it? Use recovery phrase.
  2. Type the 24 words, separated by spaces.
  3. Choose a new master password (12 characters or more), type it twice and choose Recover vault.

Afterwards set up Windows Hello and camera verification again. The recovery phrase keeps working.

Change the master password

Settings › Unlocking › Master password › Change…. Enter the current one and the new one twice. Backups you already made keep the password they were made with.

Locking

  • Lock button in the top bar, or Ctrl+L.
  • Emergency lock: Ctrl+Alt+L anywhere in Windows. It locks at once and cancels any fill in progress.
  • Settings › Locking:
    • Lock after inactivity: Never, or 1 minute up to 24 hours.
    • Lock when Windows locks (Win+L, switching user, signing out) and Lock on sleep (lid closed, sleep, hibernate).
    • Never stay unlocked longer than: a hard limit, whatever you are doing.
    • Count activity from: Computer activity or Vault activity.
    • Ask before releasing 2FA codes to the browser extension.

The tray icon menu has Unlock vault, Lock vault, Quick access and Exit.

The item list

  • Filter chips: All, Needs attention, No 2FA, Expiring.
  • Sort: Title A–Z, Title Z–A, Date modified, Date created.
  • Flags on a row show a password that is Breached, Weak or Reused.
  • Right-click a row (or Shift+F10) for Copy password, Copy username, Copy 2FA code, Open website, Favorites, Share… and Move to trash….

The detail pane

Shows the fields of the selected item. Passwords stay hidden until you click them. Buttons copy the username, password or 2FA code, open the website, Edit, Share or Move to trash. Documents add Open file, Safe preview, Save a copy… and Replace file….

Adding and editing items

  1. Choose New item or press Ctrl+N.
  2. Pick a kind (click it, press its number, or start typing its name):
    • Sign-ins: Website login, Desktop app login, Passkey
    • Personal: Payment card, Bank account, Identity, Secure note, Document, Wi-Fi network
    • Things you own: License, Purchase, Warranty, Subscription, Bookmark
  3. Fill in the fields and choose Save. If you leave with unsaved changes you are asked Discard your changes?

Logins in detail

  • Title, Website, Username, Password and Notes.
  • Change next to the password makes a new one with the generator. Change it on the website too, then save.
  • Two-factor: paste the site's setup key here (Pro). See Two-factor codes.
  • Custom fields: Add field for anything else. Mark a field Hidden to treat it like a password.
  • Password history: old passwords are kept, encrypted, for a year. Copy previous password or Restore this password.
  • Recovery codes: one per line. Copy and use next code copies one and removes it from the list.
  • Autofill rules: Filling (Default, On click, Never), Sign in after filling, Always ask for the master password, and Offer to save changes from the browser.

Other kinds

Each kind has its own fields. Dates are written as YYYY-MM-DD. Every item can be a Favorite and carry Tags (separate them with commas). Documents use Choose file and save (Pro).

Collections and reminders

  • Collections group items your way: Add collection, Rename, Archive. They stay on this PC and do not sync.
  • Reminders lists items that expire soon or are overdue, with Complete and Snooze 1 day.
  • Activity shows a history of what happened in the vault.

Deleting, trash and undo

  1. Select an item and choose Move to trash or press Delete.
  2. A message appears with Undo: click it to bring the item straight back.
  3. Later, open Tools › Trash and use Restore selected, Delete selected or Empty trash. Deleting from the trash cannot be undone.

Settings › Privacy › Keep deleted items for sets 7, 14, 30 or 90 days, and Empty trash automatically clears it for you.

Search, command palette and Quick access

  • Search: Ctrl+F, type, and press Enter to open the top result or ↓ to move into the list.
  • Command palette: Ctrl+K finds items and actions. Start with > for actions only (Back up now, Generate password, Lock now, Sync status…), # for tags and @ for collections.
  • Quick access: Ctrl+Alt+K anywhere in Windows opens a small search window for logins, notes and bookmarks. Change the shortcut in Settings › Autofill › Quick access.

Password generator

Open it from the Generator card on Home, from Change in a login, or with Ctrl+K › Generate password.

  • Random: choose the length and whether to use capitals and symbols.
  • Passphrase: choose the number of words. Easier to type, just as strong when long enough.
  • Generate again for a new one, Copy to copy it.

Security center and password health

Tools › Security center lists what to fix first: weak, reused, old and breached passwords, and logins that could have 2FA.

  • Check all for breaches compares your passwords with the Have I Been Pwned list. Only the first 5 characters of a hash leave your PC, never the password.
  • Breach checks are off until you switch on Settings › Privacy › Breach checks. Strict offline mode blocks them, website icons and every other network request.
  • On Password health use Open entry or Change password to fix an item.

Two-factor codes and QR scanning Pro

LocalGateKeys can show the 6-digit codes a site asks for after the password, so you don't need a separate authenticator app.

  1. On the website, turn on two-factor / authenticator-app sign-in so it shows a QR code.
  2. In LocalGateKeys choose Tools › Scan QR code. Bring the picture in one of three ways:
    • Paste screenshot: press Win+Shift+S, select the QR code, then Ctrl+V.
    • Choose image… (PNG, JPEG, BMP) or drag the image in.
    • Use webcam and hold a phone screen showing the code up to it.
  3. Pick the login it belongs to, or New login, then Open in editor and Save.
  4. Type the current code on the website to finish turning on 2FA there.

You can also paste the setup key or an otpauth:// link straight into the login's Two-factor section.

Moving from Google Authenticator

  1. In Google Authenticator choose Transfer accounts › Export.
  2. Scan the QR code it shows (for several codes, scan each one: "Code 1 of 3 scanned").
  3. Tick the accounts, choose Add to existing login or New login, then Add. Duplicates are skipped.

New logins made this way get the tag needs-password: fill in the real password later. Pictures are read in memory and never saved. On Free, codes you already have keep working; adding or changing them needs Pro.

Import from another password manager

  1. Export your passwords from the old app (see the list below).
  2. In LocalGateKeys open Tools › Import & export › Import passwords & items… and choose the file. The format is recognised automatically. Encrypted files ask for their Import password.
  3. A plain CSV from an unknown app shows Choose columns: tell it which column is the title, username, password and so on.
  4. Review every row: Add, Skip, Replace, Merge or Keep both. Use Add all new, Replace all conflicts or Skip everything for all at once.
  5. Choose Import. It all goes in at once; if anything fails, nothing is written.
  6. Delete the export file: it holds your passwords unencrypted.

Export and encrypted transfer

Tools › Import & export › Export… offers:

  • KeePass KDBX 4: encrypted with a password you choose (12+ characters). The safe choice.
  • Bitwarden-compatible CSV, Bitwarden JSON, KeePass 2 XML: unencrypted. Delete them after use.
  • Tick Include one-time password (2FA) secrets to take your 2FA codes along.

Encrypted transfer… moves chosen items (by favourites, kinds, tags) to another profile or PC in a password-protected package. On the other side choose Import a package, Inspect, Preview, then import.

Backups and restore

Scheduled backups (recommended)

  1. Settings › Backups › Backup password › Set…. Use 12+ characters and write it down.
  2. Scheduled backups: Daily or Weekly, with Time of day and Day of the week.
  3. Cloud copy › Choose… a folder, such as a USB drive or a second disk, for a second copy.
  4. Old scheduled backups: keep only the latest, or the latest and previous.

Backups run only while the vault is unlocked. One missed while the app was closed runs at the next unlock.

Backup & restore page

Open it from the Backup & recovery card, Ctrl+K, or Backup & restore on the unlock screen.

  • Create a backup: an encrypted .lgk file with its own password.
  • Verify a backup: checks a file opens, without touching your vault.
  • Restore backup: needs the backup password and the master password from when it was made. You can restore it as a new profile.
  • Test a restore: opens a temporary copy to prove your backup works, then Discard the test copy.
  • Recovery phrase (Set up / Replace…) and Write recovery kit, a printable sheet.

Restoring a phone backup

Import & export › Restore phone backup…, choose the .lgkm file from the iPhone or Android app, enter its password, Preview, then Restore. Items are added to your vault; nothing is wiped.

Sync between devices Pro

Sync runs only while unlocked: when you unlock, every 5 minutes, and a few seconds after a change. Two ways, and you can use both:

Wi-Fi (phone or another PC on the same network)

  1. Tools › Sync › Turn on Wi-Fi sync…. Keep the suggested address and port (47821), and choose Allow through Windows Firewall if asked.
  2. Choose Show QR code…. The code works once, for 120 seconds.
  3. Scan it with the phone app's Sync tab. For another PC, choose Copy code text and use Join by QR text… there.
  4. Both devices show a 6-digit code. Choose Accept only if they match, otherwise Decline.
  5. Right after pairing, Undo removes the new device if it was a mistake.

If the PC's address changes, choose Find again on the phone.

Cloud folder (Dropbox, iCloud Drive…)

  1. On the first device: Create sync group…, choose a folder your cloud app syncs, and set a strong sync password (12+ characters).
  2. On each other device: Join a folder…, choose the folder holding LocalGateKeys Sync, enter the sync password.

The cloud only ever sees encrypted files.

OneDrive and Google Drive are unavailable for sync for now, until an issue with Apple is resolved. Use Dropbox, iCloud Drive or Wi-Fi sync instead.

Managing sync

  • Sync now on the Sync status page (click the sync pill in the sidebar).
  • Remove a device… (sets a new sync password), Change sync password….
  • Leave sync… keeps your items and forgets the group. Reset sync on this PC… also forgets its keys (asks twice).
  • If an item changed on two devices at once, the extra copy gets (sync conflict) in its title.

Each PC needs its own Pro licence; a Pro phone purchase can unlock a PC (see Pro licence). Collections do not sync. Up to 16 devices per group.

Sharing an item with someone

  1. Select the item, choose Share, then Create share.
  2. LocalGateKeys shows a six-word Share code. Choose Save as file… (a .lgkshare) or Show QR code.
  3. Send the file, then tell the person the six words another way, such as by phone or in person.

Receiving: double-click the .lgkshare file, or Ctrl+K › Incoming shared items › Open a shared item…. Enter the six words, check the Preview, then Save to vault, Keep both or Reject. Passkeys, documents and items with an attached file cannot be shared.

Filling Windows apps

LocalGateKeys can fill sign-in windows of ordinary Windows programs, not just websites.

  1. Open the program's sign-in window.
  2. Create a Desktop app login and choose App binding… › Inspect the app….
  3. Map login fields: point at the username and password boxes. Optionally Map sign-in button….
  4. Save binding.
  5. Next time, press Ctrl+Shift+Space in that program or click the fill button next to its password box.

If the program changes after an update, choose Refresh binding. LocalGateKeys reads only the window layout, never what you type.

Install and connect

  1. Install and set up the Windows app.
  2. Add the extension: Edge from Edge Add-ons (choose Get). The Chrome Web Store listing is coming soon.
  3. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin LocalGateKeys.
  4. Click the LocalGateKeys icon. The connection card should say Connected or Locked.

There is nothing to pair: the extension finds the app on its own. The app's setup guide has a Test connection step to check it.

The extension window

Connection
Connected (ready), Locked (the app asks you to unlock when you choose an action), Setup required or Offline. ↻ refreshes it without unlocking anything.
Current site
The site you are on. Only logins saved for this exact site are offered.
Quick actions
Fill login, Fill identity, Fill card…, Save login, Generate password.
Save prompts
A switch to turn save prompts off or on for this site.

By design nothing fills by itself. Every fill starts with your click, and the extension never asks for your master password: unlocking always happens in the LocalGateKeys window.

Filling a login

  1. Open the site's sign-in page.
  2. Click the LocalGateKeys icon inside the username or password box (Fill with LocalGateKeys), or open the extension and choose Fill login.
  3. If you have several accounts for the site, pick one under Choose an account.
  4. If the vault is locked, unlock it in the LocalGateKeys window when it appears.

If the login has Sign in after filling turned on, the form is submitted for you. On unusual forms you'll see Automatic sign-in was skipped: just click the site's sign-in button.

Saving a new or changed login

  1. Sign in on a website as usual.
  2. The extension asks Save this login? (or offers to update a changed password).
  3. Choose Save in vault and confirm in the LocalGateKeys window. Or Not now, or Never on this site.

To save by hand, fill in the form and choose Save login in the extension window. To turn prompts back on for a site, use the switch in the extension window.

Filling 2FA codes

When a site asks for a 6-digit code, click the LocalGateKeys icon in the code box (Fill 2FA code with LocalGateKeys). Only the current code reaches the page, never the setup key. If the code is about to expire, the extension waits a moment for a fresh one.

The login must have a 2FA setup key saved in the app (see Two-factor codes). If Settings › Locking › Ask before releasing 2FA codes is on, confirm in the app each time.

Cards and identities

  1. On a checkout or address form, click the icon in a field or choose Fill card… / Fill identity.
  2. Pick the card or identity.
  3. Tick the fields to fill. The security code (CVV) is unticked by default: tick it only when needed.
  4. Choose Review and approve, then approve in the LocalGateKeys window.

Creating a password on a sign-up page

On a sign-up or change-password form, choose Generate password. The new password goes into the new-password boxes. Save it (the save prompt or Save login) before leaving the page.

Passkeys

When a site offers to create or use a passkey, LocalGateKeys can handle it and keep the passkey in your vault. Turn this on or off in the app: Settings › Autofill › Offer LocalGateKeys for passkeys. When it can't help, the browser's own passkey window takes over.

First start and unlocking

There is no password to create. Your vault opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode, the same as your phone. (The iPhone needs a passcode set.)

  1. Open the app and tap Unlock.
  2. A short setup follows: Unlock with Face ID, Import your passwords, Add an app password (optional) and Turn on AutoFill.
  3. Skip anything you like. A Finish setting up card at the top of the vault takes you back; Settings › Show setup again does too.

App password and recovery phrase

An optional extra password that, instead of your iPhone passcode, opens LocalGateKeys. Useful if others know your passcode.

  1. Settings › App password › turn on Use an app password.
  2. Choose a strong password.
  3. Write down the 24-word recovery phrase, tap I wrote them down, and type the three words it asks for.
  4. Optionally keep Unlock with Face ID as a shortcut.

Forgot it? Tap Forgot app password? on the unlock screen, type the 24 words, and choose a new app password.

If you add a new face or fingerprint to the iPhone, the Face ID shortcut turns off for safety: type the app password once and turn it back on.

Items: add, edit, delete

  1. On the Vault tab tap + and choose a kind: web login, secure note, card, identity, bank account, purchase, subscription, warranty, Wi-Fi, software licence, API key, SSH key or bookmark.
  2. Fill in the fields and tap Save. Leave halfway and the app offers to Continue your unsaved item later.
  • Open an item to Copy username, Copy password, Copy 2FA code, Open website, mark it Favorite or Share it. Copied text is cleared from the clipboard after a while and never goes to your other Apple devices.
  • Password history on a login keeps old passwords: copy or restore one (asks for Face ID).
  • Cards, passports, licences and subscriptions can get a reminder before they expire.
  • Attach a file (Pro, up to 25 MB, stored encrypted) from +.
  • Delete: the item goes to Recently deleted, with Undo for a few seconds. Settings › Data › Recently deleted lets you Restore or Delete forever.
  • Sort by name or date, favourites first, or grouped by type.

Search and quick actions

Pull down the vault or tap Search and type. Type > first to see Actions: New item, Generate password, Scan QR code, Lock now, Sync now, Security check, Recently deleted, Import & Export and more.

Password generator

From > Generate password, or while editing a login's password. Choose a password with a Length, or a passphrase with a number of Words and a Separator. Generate again, then Use. It remembers the options you used last; Reset to defaults brings them back.

Two-factor codes Pro

  1. On the website, turn on authenticator-app 2FA so it shows a QR code.
  2. In LocalGateKeys choose Scan QR code (from > or the login's 2FA field). Tap Use camera or Choose from Photos (for a screenshot).
  3. The code goes to the matching login, or a new login opens ready to save.

Codes appear in the item and in the Codes row of the vault. You can also paste a setup key into the login's 2FA secret field. Google Authenticator exports (Transfer accounts) work too, including multi-part ones.

Want the codes on your wrist? See Apple Watch.

AutoFill in apps and Safari

  1. In LocalGateKeys go to Settings › AutoFill › Open AutoFill settings.
  2. In the iPhone Settings, under General › AutoFill & Passwords, turn on LocalGateKeys.
  3. Now tap a username or password box in any app or website: your login appears above the keyboard, or tap the key icon to pick one.

Options in Settings › AutoFill:

  • Show logins in the QuickType bar: suggestions above the keyboard.
  • Copy 2FA code when filling: so you can paste the code on the next screen. It clears after 60 seconds.
  • Offer LocalGateKeys for passkeys.

Cards and contact details fill the same way. A login saved for an https site isn't offered on a plain http page.

Passkeys

With AutoFill on, when a website offers to create a passkey, choose LocalGateKeys. The passkey is saved in your vault and syncs to your PC or Mac. Signing in to a computer by scanning its QR code with the iPhone works too. A passkey's detail page shows the site and account; the key itself is never shown. You can rename or delete it.

Saving logins from Safari

  1. iPhone Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › LocalGateKeys: turn it on and allow it on all websites.
  2. Sign in to a site in Safari and tap Save in the prompt.
  3. Next time you unlock LocalGateKeys, Save from Safari? lists what was caught, marked New login or Updates the saved password. Choose what to keep, or Decide later.

Choose Never on this site to stop the prompt on a site.

Security tab

A score and lists of logins that are Breached, Reused, Weak, Old, or could use 2FA. Swipe left on a login for Change password or Dismiss. Breach checks are off until you turn on Settings › Allow breach checks.

Import and export

From Apple Passwords (iOS 26)

  1. Open the Passwords app, tap the menu and choose Export Data to Another App.
  2. Pick LocalGateKeys. Logins, 2FA codes and passkeys come across. Doing it again adds nothing twice.

From a file

Settings › Import & Export › Import from a file: Apple Passwords CSV, Bitwarden .json (also password-protected), 1Password .1pux, or any other CSV. You review every item before tapping Import. Delete the export file afterwards.

Backup and export

  • Export encrypted backup: a .lgkm file with a password of 12+ characters. Restore it on Windows, Mac or another phone.
  • Export as CSV: unencrypted, for moving to another app. You're asked to confirm.

Sync with your PC or Mac Pro

Over Wi-Fi

  1. On the PC or Mac, open Sync and choose Show QR code.
  2. On the iPhone, open the Sync tab and tap Scan QR code within 120 seconds. (Allow Local Network access the first time.) No camera? Tap Paste code text and paste the text from Copy code text on the computer.
  3. Check that both screens show the same code, then choose Accept on the computer.

Sync happens when both are on the same Wi-Fi and unlocked. Sync now forces it; Find again helps if the computer's address changed.

Through a cloud folder

Tap Join a folder…, pick the LocalGateKeys Sync folder in iCloud Drive or Dropbox, and enter the group's sync password.

OneDrive and Google Drive are unavailable for sync for now, until an issue with Apple is resolved. Use Dropbox, iCloud Drive or Wi-Fi sync instead.

Stopping

Leave sync… or Reset sync on this iPhone… (each asks twice). Your items stay on the phone.

Sharing an item

  1. Open the item and tap Share.
  2. The app shows six words. Send the file with AirDrop or share the file…, or Show the code as a QR.
  3. Tell the six words to the other person separately, never with the file.

Receiving: open the .lgkshare file from AirDrop or Files (or Receive a shared item), and type the six words. Passkeys and items with an attached file cannot be shared.

Create the vault

  1. Open LocalGateKeys, type a master password twice and choose Create vault.
  2. Setup follows: Set up Touch ID, Import… your passwords, Open recovery phrase, and Open Devices & connections for Safari and AutoFill.

Unlocking and locking

  • Touch ID: Settings › Unlocking › Unlock with Touch ID. If you change fingerprints, use the master password once.
  • Change master password: Settings › Unlocking. Your items stay as they are.
  • Recovery phrase: set it up in the Recovery centre and confirm three words. If you forget the master password, choose Forgot your master password? › Recover with your recovery phrase on the lock screen.
  • Lock: ⌘L. Settings › Locking sets idle lock, lock with the screen, on sleep, and a maximum session time.

Items

  • New Item (⌘N) offers the same kinds as the iPhone, plus Attach a file… (Pro).
  • Copy with ⌘C (password), ⇧⌘C (username), ⌘T (2FA code).
  • Add field for custom fields, Tags, and Collections (they stay on this Mac).
  • ⌫ moves an item to Recently deleted. Use Restore selected or Empty trash there.
  • Attached files open with Quick Look after Touch ID or the master password.
  • Generator: random password (choose character sets, avoid look-alikes) or passphrase (capitalise, add a number, separator).

Two-factor codes Pro

Choose Scan QR code…, then Choose image…, paste a screenshot with ⌘V (take one with ⇧⌘4), or drag an image in. Codes fill in Safari through AutoFill.

Security centre

Shows weak, reused and old passwords. Check all passwords looks for breaches once you turn on Settings › Privacy › Breach checks (and Strict offline mode is off).

AutoFill, passkeys and Safari

  1. Open Devices & connections.
  2. Open AutoFill settings and turn on LocalGateKeys under System Settings › AutoFill & Passwords.
  3. Open Safari settings and turn on the LocalGateKeys extension under Safari › Settings › Extensions.

Passwords, passkeys and 2FA codes then fill in Safari and apps. New sign-ins in Safari are offered for saving; review them at the next unlock. Other browsers aren't supported on the Mac yet.

Import and export

  • Apple Passwords (macOS 26): logins, 2FA and passkeys, reviewed before import.
  • Files: Bitwarden .json (also password-protected), 1Password .1pux, KeePass .kdbx, and CSV with Choose columns.
  • iPhone and Android .lgkm backups: choose Add all new, Keep both for duplicates or Skip all. Nothing is wiped.
  • Export an encrypted backup, or a CSV (unencrypted, asks first).

Backups

In the Recovery centre:

  • Full backup: Create… a .lgk file, or Restore… one (it replaces the vault). Works between Mac and Windows. A safety copy is kept, so Put back… undoes a restore.
  • Scheduled backups: how often, weekday, time, how many to keep, folder and mirror folder, and a backup password (12+ characters).
  • Recovery kit…: a printable sheet with your recovery details.

Sync Pro

  • Host Wi-Fi sync: show the pairing QR code. When a device joins, check its code and choose Accept or Decline. If you don't recognise it afterwards, choose Undo.
  • Join a Windows PC: use the code text from the PC's Copy code text.
  • Cloud folder: join or create a group in a folder such as Dropbox or iCloud Drive, with the sync password. OneDrive and Google Drive are unavailable for now, until an issue with Apple is resolved.
  • Leave sync… / Reset sync on this Mac….

Sharing

Select an item and choose Share…. To receive, choose File › Receive a Shared Item…, open or drop the file (or paste the QR text) and type the six words. Passkeys and items with an attached file cannot be shared.

Pro

LocalGateKeys for Apple Watch

Shows your two-factor codes on your wrist. It comes with the iPhone app. It holds codes only: no passwords, no editing. Pro

Coming in iPhone app 1.1. The watch app and the Show on Apple Watch button arrive with version 1.1, which is in App Review. Check your version in iPhone Settings › About.

On this tab

  1. Put a code on the watch
  2. Using the codes
  3. Removing codes

Put a code on the watch

  1. Update the iPhone app to version 1.1 or later.
  2. Install LocalGateKeys on the watch: open the Watch app on the iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to LocalGateKeys.
  3. In LocalGateKeys on the iPhone, open a login that has a 2FA code.
  4. In its One-time code section, under the code, tap Show on Apple Watch. The iPhone confirms Sent to Apple Watch.

Don't see the button? It only appears when the iPhone is paired with an Apple Watch and LocalGateKeys is installed on that watch, and only on logins that have a 2FA code. After installing the watch app, close the item and open it again.

Up to 10 codes can be on the watch. Without Pro, tapping the button explains that watch codes are part of Pro.

Using the codes

Open LocalGateKeys on the watch. The Codes screen lists each site with its current code and a countdown. Codes keep working without the iPhone nearby.

Removing codes

On the iPhone, open the login and tap Remove from Apple Watch. To clear all watch codes whenever the iPhone app locks, turn on iPhone Settings › Forget Apple Watch codes on lock.

First start and unlocking

Your vault opens with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock. (The phone needs a screen lock.) A short setup follows: unlock with fingerprint, Open Autofill settings, Import… and an optional app password. Skip any step; the Finish setting up card brings you back.

Settings › Security sets Auto-lock after leaving the app (Immediately to 1 hour, or Never) and has Lock now. The app is hidden in the recent-apps view.

App password and recovery phrase

  1. Settings › Turn on app password and choose one. From then on it, not the screen lock, opens LocalGateKeys.
  2. Write down the 24-word recovery phrase, tap I wrote them down, and type the words it asks for.

Forgot it? Tap Forgot app password?, type the 24 words, and choose a new one. Change and Turn off are in Settings.

After restoring a phone from a Google backup, the vault can't be opened (its key isn't backed up). Choose Start fresh, then sync with your PC to get everything back.

Items

  • Tap + and pick a kind: Website login, Note, Payment card, Identity, Bank account, Purchase, Subscription, Warranty, Wi-Fi network, License, API key, SSH key, Bookmark.
  • Open an item to copy fields, Open website, Edit, Delete or Add to Favorites.
  • Password history: copy or restore an earlier password.
  • Add a file (Pro): take a photo, choose a photo or a file, up to 25 MB each.
  • Sort by title or date, favourites first.

Search and actions

On the Search tab type a name, or start with > for actions such as Export backup, Generate password, Import passwords, Lock, Receive shared item, Scan QR code to pair and Sync now.

Password generator

Password (length, upper and lower case, digits, symbols, avoid look-alikes) or Passphrase (words, capitalise, add a number, separator). Generate again, then Copy.

Two-factor codes Pro

Edit a login and tap Scan QR code next to the 2FA field, or type the setup key. Codes show in the item and in the Codes row: tap one to copy it.

Autofill

  1. Settings › Autofill › Open Autofill settings and choose LocalGateKeys. (If it doesn't open, search the phone's Settings for Autofill service.)
  2. Tap a sign-in box in any app or website and pick your login from the suggestions. Unlock when asked.
  3. After signing in somewhere new, choose Save on Save login?, or Update password when it changed.

Passkeys

Turn on Settings › Offer LocalGateKeys for passkeys. When a site or app offers a passkey, choose LocalGateKeys; sign in later with Use your passkey.

Import

Import from another app ›Choose export file: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Apple Passwords, Bitwarden .json (also password-protected), 1Password .1pux, or any CSV. Review everything, then import. Delete the export file afterwards.

Backups

Export backup… saves an encrypted .lgkm file with a password you choose (it can't be recovered). Restore from a backup… opens one, shows a preview, and never overwrites a newer item. Works with the PC and iPhone too.

Sync with your PC Pro

  1. On the PC choose Show QR code on the Wi-Fi card.
  2. On the phone, Sync tab › Scan QR code (or Paste code text).
  3. Check both show the same code and accept on the PC.

Or Join a folder… in Dropbox or Nextcloud with the sync password. The PC needs Pro too.

OneDrive and Google Drive are unavailable for sync for now, until an issue with Apple is resolved. Use Dropbox, Nextcloud or Wi-Fi sync instead.

Sharing

Open an item › Share this item › Send as a file… or Show as a QR code, and tell the six words separately. To receive: Receive a shared item, enter the six words, then Add to vault. The share opens once within 24 hours; three wrong codes delete it. Passkeys and files cannot be shared.
