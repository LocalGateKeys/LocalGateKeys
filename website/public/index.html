How to get started

You decide where encrypted copies go

Every device keeps its own encrypted vault. Nothing leaves a device unless you set it up to. Sync between your PCs is part of Pro.

Nowhere

The default, in every edition

The vault stays on this PC. Scheduled backups are encrypted files you keep where you like, including a USB drive or a sync folder.

Your home Wi-Fi

Pro

Pair your PCs once with a one-use pairing code. When they are on the same network and unlocked, they sync directly with each other over an encrypted connection. No internet connection is involved.

Your own cloud folder

Pro, if you turn it on

Point sync at a OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive or iCloud Drive folder you already use, protected by a sync password. Your cloud provider stores encrypted files it cannot read. We are not involved at all.

Fills sign-ins in Chrome and Edge

The browser extension talks to the LocalGateKeys app on your PC through a small local helper. It only offers logins saved for the exact site you are on. It also fills two-factor codes, addresses and payment cards, and offers to save new logins after you sign in.

Passkeys work too: a website can save a passkey in your vault and sign you in with it, and you approve each one in the desktop app.

The extension never receives your whole vault, and no request leaves your computer.

And in Windows apps

Desktop app fill works with sign-in windows of Windows programs such as game launchers. You bind a window once; after that a fill button beside the password field, or a hotkey, fills the saved fields.

It reads only the accessibility information Windows exposes about a window. It never records your keystrokes, never injects code into another program, and refuses to fill if the app or window has changed.

Two-factor codes built in

Save a site's two-factor setup key with its login and LocalGateKeys shows the current code at the top of the login. A One-time codes card on the Home page lists them all. Add a key by pasting a snip of the site's QR code, holding it up to your webcam, or from a Google Authenticator export.

Codes are worked out on your PC. Two-factor codes are part of Pro.

Checks your passwords

The generator makes strong passwords or passphrases. Password health shows which saved passwords are weak, reused, old or missing a two-factor code. If you turn it on, a breach check you start compares them with known breaches, sending only five characters of a hash.
