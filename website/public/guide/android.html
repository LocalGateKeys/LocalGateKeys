Good to know on every device

  • Your vault stays on your devices. There is no account and no LocalGateKeys server holding your passwords.
  • Nobody can reset your master password or app password. Set up a recovery phrase and keep it on paper.
  • Free holds up to 7 items. Pro (US$29.99, paid once) adds unlimited items, two-factor codes, files and sync. Editing, deleting and filling items you already have are never blocked.

First start and unlocking

Your vault opens with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock. (The phone needs a screen lock.) A short setup follows: unlock with fingerprint, Import…, an optional app password and Open Autofill settings. Skip any step; the Finish setting up card brings you back.

Settings › Lock & security sets Auto-lock after leaving the app (Immediately to 1 hour) and Lock when idle (1 to 30 minutes, or Never), and has Lock now. The app is hidden in the recent-apps view.

App password and recovery phrase

  1. Settings › Lock & security › turn on Use an app password, choose one and tap Continue. From then on it, not the screen lock, opens LocalGateKeys.
  2. Write down the 24-word recovery phrase, tap I wrote them down, type the words it asks for and tap Turn on app password.

Forgot it? Tap Forgot app password?, type the 24 words, and choose a new one. Change app password, and the Use an app password switch to turn it off, are in Settings › Lock & security.

After restoring a phone from a Google backup, the vault can't be opened (its key isn't backed up). Choose Start fresh, then sync with your PC to get everything back.

Items

  • Tap + and pick a kind: Website login, Note, Payment card, Identity, Bank account, Purchase, Subscription, Warranty, Wi-Fi network, License, API key, SSH key, Bookmark.
  • Open an item to copy fields, Open website, Edit, Delete or Add to Favorites.
  • Password history: copy or restore an earlier password.
  • Add a file (Pro): take a photo, choose a photo or a file, up to 25 MB each.
  • Sort by title or date, favourites first.

Search and actions

Tap the magnifier on the Vault tab and type a name, or start with > for actions such as Export backup, Generate password, Import passwords, Lock, Receive shared item, Scan QR code to pair and Sync now.

Password generator

Password (length, upper and lower case, digits, symbols, avoid look-alikes) or Passphrase (words, capitalise, add a number, separator). Generate again, then Copy.

Two-factor codes Pro

Edit a login and tap Scan QR code next to the 2FA field, or type the setup key. Codes show in the item and in the Codes row: tap one to copy it.

Autofill

  1. Settings › Autofill › Open Autofill settings and choose LocalGateKeys. (If it doesn't open, search the phone's Settings for Autofill service.)
  2. Tap a sign-in box in any app or website and pick your login from the suggestions. Unlock when asked.
  3. After signing in somewhere new, choose Save on Save login?, or Update password when it changed.

Passkeys

Turn on Settings › Autofill › Offer LocalGateKeys for passkeys. When a site or app offers a passkey, choose LocalGateKeys; sign in later with Use your passkey.

Import

Settings › Sync & data › Import from a file › Choose export file: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Apple Passwords, Bitwarden .json (also password-protected), 1Password .1pux, or any CSV. Review everything, then import. Delete the export file afterwards.

Backups

Export backup… saves an encrypted .lgkm file with a password you choose (it can't be recovered). Restore from a backup… opens one, shows a preview, and never overwrites a newer item. Works with the PC and iPhone too.

Sync with your PC Pro

  1. On the PC choose Show QR code on the Wi-Fi card.
  2. On the phone, Sync tab › Scan QR code (or Paste code text).
  3. Check both show the same code and accept on the PC.

Or Join a folder… in Dropbox or Nextcloud with the sync password. The PC needs Pro too.

OneDrive and Google Drive are unavailable for sync for now, until an issue with Apple is resolved. Use Dropbox, Nextcloud or Wi-Fi sync instead.

Sharing

Open an item, tap Share, then Send as a file… or Show as a QR code, and tell the six words separately. To receive: Settings › Sync & data › Receive a shared item, enter the six words, tap Open, then Add to vault. The share opens once within 24 hours; three wrong codes delete it. Passkeys and files cannot be shared.

Settings and Pro

  • Appearance: System, Light or Dark, and your initials.
  • Lock & security has Clear clipboard after, Privacy has Hide passwords until tapped, and Sync & data has Erase this phone's vault (this phone only). Android lets the app check the clipboard only while it is open, so a copy made from the Quick Settings tile or just before you switch apps is cleared the next time you open LocalGateKeys.
  • Pro (top of Settings): Upgrade and buy once through Google Play, Restore purchases on another phone with the same Google account, and Copy key to unlock Windows too.
  • Duress password (Pro): a second app password for when someone forces you to unlock. It opens a separate vault with only the items you choose, and your vault then refuses its app password for the hours you set. Turn on the app password first, then set it up under Duress password in Settings.
  • Export to a CXF file… writes the logins, notes and passkeys you pick to a Credential Exchange file that other password managers can import. The file is not encrypted.
