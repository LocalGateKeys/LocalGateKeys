LocalGateKeys for Mac

Step-by-step help for every screen and feature on this device. Every heading is a link you can share.

Good to know on every device

  • Your vault stays on your devices. There is no account and no LocalGateKeys server holding your passwords.
  • Nobody can reset your master password or app password. Set up a recovery phrase and keep it on paper.
  • Free holds up to 7 items. Pro (US$29.99, paid once) adds unlimited items, two-factor codes, files and sync. Editing, deleting and filling items you already have are never blocked.

Mac topics

The sidebar lists your items (All items, Favourites, Recently used, each kind and your collections) and the tools Security centre, Generator, Sync & devices, Import & export and Trash, with Settings at the bottom. The Recovery centre opens from Settings › Backups, and Devices & connections from Sync & devices.

Create the vault

  1. Open LocalGateKeys, type a master password twice and choose Create vault.
  2. Setup follows: Set up Touch ID, Import… your passwords, Open recovery phrase, and Open Devices & connections for Safari and AutoFill.

Unlocking and locking

  • Touch ID: Settings › Security › Unlocking › Touch ID. If you change fingerprints, use the master password once.
  • Change master password: Settings › Unlocking. Your items stay as they are.
  • Recovery phrase: set it up in the Recovery centre and confirm three words. If you forget the master password, choose Forgot your master password? › Recover with your recovery phrase on the lock screen.
  • Lock: ⌘L. Settings › Locking sets idle lock, lock with the screen, on sleep, and a maximum session time. You can also choose what counts as activity for idle lock.

Items

  • New Item (⌘N) offers the same kinds as the iPhone, plus Attach a file… (Pro).
  • Copy with ⌘C (password), ⇧⌘C (username), ⌘T (2FA code).
  • Add field for custom fields, Tags, and Collections (they sync with your other devices).
  • ⌫ moves an item to Recently deleted. Open Trash in the sidebar and use Restore selected or Empty trash.
  • Attached files open with Quick Look after Touch ID or the master password.
  • Generator: random password (choose character sets, avoid look-alikes) or passphrase (capitalise, add a number, separator).

Two-factor codes Pro

Choose Scan QR code…, then Choose image…, paste a screenshot with ⌘V (copy one with ⌃⇧⌘4), or drag an image in. Codes fill in Safari through AutoFill.

Security centre

Shows weak, reused and old passwords. Check all passwords looks for breaches once you turn on Settings › Privacy › Breach checks (and Strict offline mode is off).

AutoFill, passkeys and Safari

  1. Open Devices & connections.
  2. Open AutoFill settings and turn on LocalGateKeys under System Settings › AutoFill & Passwords.
  3. Open Safari settings and turn on the LocalGateKeys extension under Safari › Settings › Extensions.

To fill a sign-in in Safari, click the username or password box. Safari lists your LocalGateKeys logins below it: choose one and confirm with Touch ID or your master password. Passkeys and 2FA codes are offered the same way, and AutoFill works in other Mac apps too.

The Safari extension offers to save a new sign-in after you log in. Review what it caught the next time you unlock LocalGateKeys. Other browsers aren't supported on the Mac yet.

No logins listed? Open Devices & connections and check that AutoFill is on. On the same System Settings page you can turn off Passwords, so only LocalGateKeys offers logins.

If LocalGateKeys quits when you open Devices & connections, update it from the Mac App Store.

Import and export

  • Apple Passwords (macOS 26): logins, 2FA and passkeys, reviewed before import.
  • Files: Bitwarden .json (also password-protected), 1Password .1pux, KeePass .kdbx, and CSV with Choose columns.
  • iPhone and Android .lgkm backups: choose Add all new, Keep both for duplicates or Skip all. Nothing is wiped.
  • Export an encrypted backup, or a CSV (unencrypted, asks first).

Backups

In the Recovery centre:

  • Full backup: Create… a .lgk file, or Restore… one (it replaces the vault). Works between Mac and Windows. A safety copy is kept, so Put back… undoes a restore.
  • Scheduled backups: how often, weekday, time, how many to keep, folder and mirror folder, and a backup password (12+ characters).
  • Recovery kit…: a file that describes your last backup, to keep with it. Print emergency kit… prints a page that says where your vault and backups are and how to restore them.

Sync Pro

  • Wi-Fi (this network): Turn on Wi-Fi sync, then Show QR code…. When a device joins, check its code and choose Accept or Decline. If you don't recognise it afterwards, choose Undo.
  • Join with a code: on the other device choose Show QR code, then Copy code text, and paste the text here.
  • Cloud folder: join or create a group in a folder such as Dropbox or iCloud Drive, with the sync password. OneDrive and Google Drive are unavailable for now, until an issue with Apple is resolved.
  • Leave sync… / Reset sync on this Mac….

Sharing

Select an item and choose Share…. To receive, choose Edit › Receive a Shared Item… (or find it with ⌘K), open or drop the file (or paste the QR text) and type the six words. Passkeys and items with an attached file cannot be shared.

Pro

Buy Pro in Settings › Plan. One purchase covers both the iPhone and Mac apps; use Restore purchases on the other device. A free Mac synced with a Pro iPhone shows Pro from your synced devices.

Settings

Settings is the same in the main window and with ⌘,, and the Tools stay visible at the bottom of the sidebar. Settings › Backups holds the backup schedule, the Weekly restore test and how many automatic backups to keep; Recovery centre, Licence and Show setup again open from either window. Other settings: what counts as activity for auto-lock, asking before copying a 2FA code, opening documents stored in the vault in other apps, your own Quick access shortcut, and Help links.

Duress password (Pro), in Settings › Duress password: a second master password for when someone forces you to unlock. It opens a separate vault with only the items you choose, and your vault then refuses its master password for the hours you set.
